# Homepage Design Notes

This page inherits `design-system/MASTER.md`.

## Page Goal

Position Jonah Wambua as a Nairobi-based full-stack developer, UI/UX designer, and graphic designer who can move from strategy and interface design through production implementation.

## Section Direction

### Hero

- Lead with clear positioning, not a vague creative tagline.
- Establish location and availability.
- Use subtle kinetic typography in the H1.
- Keep primary CTA to selected work and secondary CTA to contact.
- Use the portrait as a first-viewport brand signal on large screens, but keep mobile copy unobstructed.

### Intro/About

- Add a short origin story and operating values.
- Make credibility specific: systems thinking, client work, mentoring, product/interface craft.
- Keep capability cards concise and scannable.

### Work

- Present projects as case studies, not only screenshots.
- Each card should show category, role, outcome, description, stack, and links.
- Stronger hierarchy is preferred over a dense equal-weight gallery.

### Articles

- Treat articles as field notes that show design/development thinking.
- Use clean editorial layout, restrained imagery, and clear external link affordances.

### Contact

- Make the CTA direct and conversion-focused.
- Explain what kind of work is a good fit.
- Preserve the existing Formcarry integration.
- Keep email and location alternatives visible.

## Motion Direction

- Use scroll-triggered reveal for chapter headings, copy, cards, and article items.
- Use one subtle hero parallax layer on desktop.
- Use hover lift and border/accent transitions on cards and buttons.
- Keep all motion disabled or effectively instant under reduced motion.

## Theme Direction

- Light: near-white neutral base, white surfaces, ink text, blue CTA.
- Dark: deep neutral base, elevated dark surfaces, bright readable text, softer blue accent.
- Keep borders visible in both themes.

## Content Tone

Professional, grounded, and craft-focused. Avoid hype, gimmicks, emojis, and generic agency language.
